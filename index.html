GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Here's what to know about Academic Tracker.

Who is Academic Tracker for?

It supports personal academic tracking for secondary-school and university students. It is not presented as a full school-management system.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The interface is responsive and the existing tracker is designed for mobile and desktop screens.

Will it work without internet?

The tracker includes offline-first local storage and a service worker. Account sign-in and registration need an internet connection.

Where are my academic records stored?

Personal tracker records are stored locally on your device in the current offline-first tracker. Export a JSON backup regularly. Do not assume local academic records automatically sync across devices through account sign-in.

Can I customise tests and exams?

Yes. Assessment setup is designed to let you add, edit, or remove assessments, set maximum marks, and apply a structure across subjects or courses. The default structure can differ between secondary school and university.

Does it support GPA and CGPA?

The university tracking experience includes GPA/CGPA alongside course performance. Calculations depend on the grades and course data you enter.

What happens if I forget a mark?

Leave it blank until you confirm the result. Missing marks are tracked separately so they are not silently treated as zero.
