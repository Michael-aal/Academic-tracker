How the calculation works

Default score bands: 70–100 = A/5, 60–69 = B/4, 50–59 = C/3, 45–49 = D/2, 40–44 = E/1, below 40 = F/0. GPA = total (credit units × grade points) ÷ completed credit units. Your institution may use different rules, so confirm the grading scale in its handbook before relying on these estimates.

Default assessment setup: one CA worth 30 marks and one final exam worth 70. Add tests or assignments per course as needed; the maximum contributions must total 100. Confirm your institution’s official assessment weighting before relying on results.
